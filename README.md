# 🎁 FNP Sales Analysis | Excel Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

FNP Sales Analysis is a data analytics project developed using Microsoft Excel to analyze sales performance, customer purchasing behavior, and business trends in a gift retail business.

The project explores order transactions, product categories, customer demographics, and delivery information to uncover meaningful insights into revenue generation, product performance, and customer preferences.

## 📊 Dataset Overview

The dataset contains information about:

- Customer demographics and locations
- Product names, categories, and prices
- Order quantities and transaction details
- Order dates and monthly sales trends
- Customer purchasing locations
- Gift occasions and seasonal demand
- Delivery dates and delivery duration
- Customer gender
- Order times and payment-independent sales patterns

## 🔍 Key Analysis Areas

- Monthly revenue trends
- Top 10 best-selling products
- Revenue by product category
- Sales performance across cities
- Revenue distribution by customer gender
- Sales analysis by gift occasion
- Revenue by day of the week
- Hourly sales performance
- Total revenue and number of orders
- Average order value
- Average delivery time

- Analyze overall sales and revenue performance
